The default casting rate is €0.20/g. With the default Ilios labor/material multiplier of 2, its wholesale-price contribution is €0.40/g. Casting uses basic plus secondary casting weight; component and skip-casting exclusions and per-product manual labor overrides remain in place.

In desktop and mobile **Ρυθμίσεις**, **Κόστη παραγωγής & Ilios Formula** controls casting, plating, STX technician cost, all technician tier boundaries and rates, the Ilios labor/material multiplier, the weight surcharge, the retail-label multiplier, and the price-rounding step. Existing silver-price and label-dimension controls remain available. Policy edits require an approved administrator; zero cost rates are valid, tier boundaries must increase, and multipliers must be positive.

Save the settings first. The separate **Ενημέρωση τιμών σε όλο τον κατάλογο** preview reads a consistent, current cloud snapshot and includes masters even when they have variants, including empty-suffix variants. It recalculates active costs, master draft costs, and automatic Ilios selling prices. Manual selling prices are protected by default; an explicit checkbox replaces them with formula prices and resets their manual flags. Labor overrides remain protected in either mode. STX selling prices remain unchanged, and reserved service codes `000`, `001`, and `SP` are excluded. Imported labor retains its existing stored rate/total semantics. Plating totals are recomputed from a global policy only when plating has been explicitly configured, so editing casting alone leaves legacy plating totals intact.

Applying a preview requires an online connection. The database rejects stale previews and applies every row in one transaction using the caller's permissions. Exact before/after values are recorded in `pricing_recalculation_runs`; a failed transaction leaves no partial catalogue changes. Orders, offers and legal-document prices are not rewritten. The application reads back all changed fields before reporting verification and refreshes the catalogue cache. General settings edits and offline queue replays omit unchanged pricing policies, protecting policies saved by another device.

The legacy selling-price screen contained an automatic backfill that inferred manual overrides from differences against the current formula. Merely opening the screen could therefore flag stale automatic prices after a rate/formula change. That effect and its inference helper have been removed: manual flags now come from explicit price editing. No provenance was stored for legacy flags, so their individual origins cannot be determined from the flags alone.
